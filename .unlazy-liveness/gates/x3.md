# Gates: X3 — the replay half of §7 (found by X2, reported not fixed)

X2 armed the dedupe: a replayed line is refused, proven by mutation (4 frames for 2 lines
when the stamp is deleted). That is "at most once".

The other half — "replay exactly the gap the hub names" — is unreachable in production:

    server.ts:3017
      ws.send(registerAck(message, streams.ingestedFor(peekInstances(message.payload))))

`peekInstances(payload)` is the daemon's own `supervisor.instanceIds`, which is EMPTY
immediately after a restart. So the ack a returning agent reattaches on carries `{}`, and
custody follows from head instead of replaying the named gap. Frames produced during the
absence are dropped rather than recovered — the honest-loss rule, i.e. today's behaviour.

**The circle X2 documented**, which is why it did not fix it:
  - for a useful `ingestedFor`, the daemon must name the sessiond-held ids in `instances`
  - but if it names them, `settleInstances` treats them as live and the hub stops sending
    the `restore` spawns
  - and those spawns are the daemon's ONLY source of a surviving child's cwd, because
    sessiond's list/welcome returns procId/pid/alive/head and no cwd
    (`packages/core/src/sessiond.ts:86-93`)

One candidate the leaf should weigh: **sessiond already holds the cwd** — it is in the
`spec` it was spawned with (`spawn {procId, spec{command,args,cwd,env}}`, design §3.4).
`SessiondProcInfo` simply does not expose it. Widening that would break the circle without
a second register. Other routes (hub sends the ledger for every instance on the machine;
or a second register after adoption) are open — the leaf decides and defends.
